Allergen Guide

Eat with confidence.

We take food allergies, dietary requirements and coeliac disease seriously. Please read the important information below, and always tell a member of staff about your allergy before ordering.

Important information

  • This guide covers the 14 allergens specified by UK Food Information Regulations, and shows which dishes are suitable for vegetarian and vegan diets.
  • We do not carry across "may contain" warnings from our suppliers. Our busy kitchens handle numerous ingredients and allergens — whilst we have controls in place to reduce the risk of cross-contact, we cannot guarantee any dish is allergen free.
  • Allergen segregation is not possible in our fryers.
  • Please check this guide every time you order, as dish descriptions don't always mention every ingredient, and recipes can change.
  • Allergens contained within condiments are not included in this information.
  • Our vegan dishes are made to a vegan recipe, but due to cross-contact at our suppliers and in our kitchens, they are not necessarily suitable for those with allergies.
  • Our restaurant teams cannot help select dishes and do not hold full ingredient information beyond this guide. If you can't find a dish suitable for your dietary requirements, we advise against ordering it — but as you know your allergy best, the choice is yours. You can also email us for more information.
