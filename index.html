Our Story

Two brothers, one family kitchen.

Rasoi Indian Kitchen opened in 2011 — a converted village pub in Pontlliw, built by brothers Jas and Suki Kullar while still in their teens. Family recipes, summers in Punjab, and the belief that Indian cooking deserved a modern stage. Fourteen years on, this is still where it all begins.

Charity

Beyond the table.

Rasoi was built on the idea that hospitality should reach further than the table. Every booking, every takeaway, every shared plate quietly funds something else — over 160 children sponsored through school, more than 100,000 meals served across India and Wales, free medical care and eye camps for those who otherwise have no access. When you sit with us, you're already part of it.
